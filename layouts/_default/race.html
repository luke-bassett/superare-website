The Course

Hagg Lake Circuit

The Banana Belt Road Race is a classic Pacific Northwest circuit race held on the roads surrounding scenic Henry Hagg Lake in Washington County. The course is a 10.3-mile loop with approximately 900 feet of elevation gain per lap — rolling and fast with a mix of technical corners and open stretches.
